## 1. Storytelling / script patterns

### 1.1 Titles and packaging
- **Time contract:** "Give me 9 mins. I'll make you…", "give me 5 minutes. I'll save 5 years of your life."
- **Age targeting:** "I'm 25. If you're 15–24…", "If you're between 16 to 20 years old…"
- **Brutal / secret / dangerous words:** "14 Brutally Honest Truths", "5 Secrets", "The Most Dangerous SKILL".
- **Money and numbers:** "₹2 Lakhs/Month", "My 9 Income Sources at 25", "First CRORE at 23".
- **One emoji at the end:** 🔥 (most often), ⚡, 💔, 😢. One or two words in ALL CAPS for emphasis.

### 1.2 Hook (first 5–15 seconds)
He uses one of these four openers:
1. **Contrarian quote:** "Only a fool waits for motivation… This is a very famous quote, but I don't think this is completely true."
2. **Stakes on both sides:** "Master this one skill → lakhs every month. Suck at it → your life would be hell."
3. **Shock story with a big number (Shorts):** "The story of this 18-year-old will blow your mind… 500 crores in 18 months."
4. **Direct age call-out (Shorts):** "If you're 16 to 20, these are the 3 skills you must learn before this year ends."

Straight after the hook come a **promise** ("give me the next 10 minutes, I'll show you…") and **exclusivity**
("I've never shared this with anyone… for the first time I'm sharing all my secrets"). Then he says **"Let's start."**

### 1.3 Credibility in one line
"Hi, if you're new here, my name is Tharun, I'm 25…" Then a short **personal anecdote** that frames the
whole video, e.g. a colleague asking "how are you so motivated?". The video becomes his answer to that question.

### 1.4 Body structure
- A **numbered list with a progress counter** on screen ("1/6", "3/6"), or **modules** for longer videos
  ("divided into five modules, 2–3 min each").
- Each point follows the same loop:
  1. **Question to the viewer** — "Do you know the most tiring task for your brain?"
  2. **Wrong guess** — "You might be thinking it's studying…"
  3. **Reveal** — "But it's actually taking decisions."
  4. **Personal proof** — a story with specifics (Zomato orders, the exam he almost failed, a 7-day tracking experiment).
  5. **Coined name** — "upper circuit", "regret diary", "ARE framework", "preloaded motivation".
  6. **One-line takeaway** — "A good habit is motivation you've already paid for."
  7. **Homework** — "For the next 10 days, note it down." / "Pause the video and write it in the comments."
- **Science or physics analogies:** "motivation is like potential energy".
- **Borrowed proof clips:** a Steve Jobs pause, a stand-up comedian's punchline.
- **Pattern breaks:** a self-aware joke ("Yeah. Anyways."), and warnings like "this only works if you're mentally strong, else skip".

### 1.5 Ending
- A quick recap ("these are the six things…").
- **One comment CTA with a constraint:** "Not five, not 10 — just one single goal. Write it in the comments."
- **Future callback:** "I'll take an update in my New Year's video."
- **An emotional monologue** in short, rising lines: "You'll stop caring what others think… You'll finally
  realise you're responsible…" It closes on a quotable line: "Just because you're born in a middle-class family
  doesn't mean you have to die in one."

### 1.6 Shorts formula (60–85 seconds, about 2.5–2.8 spoken words per second)
`Age call-out or shock story → FOMO ("people who master this will crush you") → "here are N things, in this exact order"
→ fast numbered list → contrarian line ("most people jump straight to level 5…") → lead magnet ("watch till the end / scan this QR")
→ share CTA ("share with the friend you want to grow with")`

### 1.7 Voice
Simple Indian English, short sentences, spoken fillers kept on purpose ("cuz", "literally", "like", "I mean").
He talks to one person ("you"), and often teases the viewer gently ("look at your own desk… is it actually clean?").

## 2. Editing patterns

### 2.1 A-roll (talking head)
- Home-office set: warm practical lamps, a poster in the background ("NOT EVERYTHING HAS A MEANING"), dark
  furniture and shallow depth of field. Sharp 4K with a warm grade.
- **Punch-ins** between sentences (wide → medium → close) instead of jump cuts on the same framing.
- Hands are very active and gestures drive the timing; text often appears on the gesture.

### 2.2 Signature effect: kinetic text *behind* the subject
Huge headline words are masked behind his head and body (depth/rotoscope effect): "THARUN", "HABITS",
"THREAT", "COMMUNICATION". There's usually a small lead-in line above in a lighter weight ("my name is",
"Build a few strong", "If something was a"). This is the most recognisable part of his style.

### 2.3 Motion-graphic cutaways (every concept gets a visual)
- **Data viz:** glowing bar charts ("DIFFICULTY 20/60/100%"), a progress slider card ("Output of Work"),
  a timeline axis ("Working hours: 4 / 6 / 8 hours") and an upper-circuit bar.
- **Icons:** a single centred icon on a flat background (a pink brain on red).
- **UI mockups:** macOS calendar windows (May/June/July), glass-style cards, chat bubbles with
  **typing-text animation** (prompt cards like "Talk to me for 10 minutes like…").
- **Structure graphics:** 3D folder icons on a glowing timeline, one per module ("SPEAKING HABITS").
- **Framework diagrams:** a glowing triangle with nodes at the corners ("ARE").
- **Illustrations:** AI-style illustrated scenes (red car on a bridge at night, an anime avatar in a circle with a speech bubble).
- **Textured b-roll:** an old paper "regret diary" with a heavy colour tint.
- **Split screen:** the graph fills the left half while he talks on the right.
- **Progress counter** at the top left ("1/6", "3/6") in bold condensed white.

### 2.4 Shorts-specific
- **Captions:** 1–3 words at a time, white, bold rounded sans, sentence case, soft shadow, centred
  slightly below the middle of the frame. No boxes and no colour highlights.
- **Hook title card** on the first frame: a small line plus a giant heavy word ("Don't Ignore These / **3 SKILLS**").
- **Mixed-scale typography:** "spend **30 MINS** everyday" (the number word is huge, the rest is small).
- Small **3D icons with labels** pop in next to his head ("GREAT COMMUNICATION").
- Full-screen cutaways: B/W photos of the person in the story, an **orbit diagram** of AI-tool logos around
  a face, neon purple 3D hands holding a "Landing Page" orb with steps 2–5 waiting, a QR code on white,
  and coloured 3D chips along the bottom edge.
- Something new appears on screen roughly **every 2–3 seconds** (estimated from the frames sampled).

### 2.5 Colour
- **One theme colour per video, plus black and white, plus glow.**
  - Motivation video: **deep crimson → black** gradients, white glowing bars and red accents.
  - Communication video: **deep navy / space black** with star particles, a **warm gold glow** (dots, diamond)
    and **electric blue** for diagrams.
  - Build Short: **purple → blue neon** on black, with white minimal screens.
- Graphics backgrounds are never flat: vignette, subtle grid or blueprint lines, particles or haze.
- A-roll is warm (tungsten) and graphics are cool or saturated, so each cutaway feels like a "scene change".

### 2.6 Fonts (closest free matches)
| Use | His look | Closest free font |
|---|---|---|
| Kinetic headline behind subject | Ultra-bold condensed caps, sometimes chrome or gradient fill | **Bebas Neue**, **Anton**, **Oswald Bold** |
| Lead-in line above headline | Clean geometric sans, medium | **Inter**, **Poppins Medium** |
| Quote subtitles (long videos) | Handwritten small caps in quotes ("ONLY A FOOL…") | **Gochi Hand**, **Permanent Marker** |
| Shorts captions | Rounded bold sans, white | **Poppins SemiBold**, **Montserrat Bold** |
| UI cards / prompts | Apple system UI | **SF Pro**, or **Inter** |
| Counter "1/6" | Bold condensed | **Bebas Neue** |

### 2.7 Music and sound (inferred, not heard)
- The captions mark `[music]` right after the hook ("Let's start") and at **every point transition**, which
  points to a **music rise or sting at each section change**.
- Some `[music]` marks also sit **under punchlines** and emotional lines, which suggests a soft bed that swells
  on the takeaway line.
- The ending is a **motivational crescendo**: a building cinematic or ambient track under the monologue.
- The "Best Skills" Short ends on a **sung hook** ("…start to mash the competition"), so Shorts can end on a
  trending, high-energy vocal track.
- From the frame cuts, the usual SFX are likely: whoosh on cutaways, pop on icon pop-ins, keyboard typing on prompt cards,
  and a riser into each "Number X".
